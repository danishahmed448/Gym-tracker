NUTRITION & RECOVERY TELEMETRY

• 1800 Kcal Target: Moderate 400–600 kcal deficit relative to 2300 kcal maintenance. Aim to lose 0.5–1% bodyweight/week (≈0.35–0.7 kg); faster cuts cost muscle.
• 130g → 145g Protein Ramp: ≈2 g/kg, top of the 1.6–2.2 g/kg range that best protects muscle in a deficit. Split over 3–5 meals. Ramp up gradually to respect upper reference uric acid levels. Hydrate with minimum 3.5L water/day.
• 8–10k Steps Daily: Burns extra fat without eating into recovery. Sunday (gym closed) = long easy walk.
• Sleep 7–9 h: Short sleep while dieting shifts weight loss from fat toward muscle.
• Creatine Monohydrate 3–5 g/day: Best-supported supplement for muscle & strength. Clear it with your doctor first given the uric acid monitoring.

HYPERTROPHY RULES (EVIDENCE-BASED)

• 6-Day PPL, Mon–Sat: Every muscle trained 2×/week at ~10–20 hard sets/week. Sunday is full rest (gym closed).
• Effort: Compounds at RIR 1–2, isolations at RIR 0–1 (last set may reach failure). Sets far from failure barely count.
• Train the Stretch: Deep ranges and long-length exercises (incline curls, overhead extensions, seated leg curls, deep flyes) build more muscle.
• Rest: 2–3 min on big lifts, 60–90 s on isolations. Very short rests on compounds reduce growth.
• Double Progression: Hit the top of the rep range on every set → the app adds weight for next session.
• Tempo & Warm-Up: Control the lowering (~2 s), no bouncing. Do 2 ramp-up sets (~50%, ~75%) before the first lift.
• Deload When Needed: Reps drop 2 sessions in a row or joints ache → 1 week at half the sets, same weights.
